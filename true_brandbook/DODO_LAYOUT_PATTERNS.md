# Dodo — паттерны макетов и брифы под форматы

> Автоизвлечено из анализа реальных макетов Dodo (кампании 2024–2026, ~452 превью). Источник правил для промпта генератора Good Brandbook. Хэнд-мейд правки допустимы — держать в синхроне с промптом.

## Общие паттерны (справедливо для всех форматов)

- ONE focal idea per layout, always. Every format is built on a single hero and ruthless single-focus hierarchy — never two co-equal focal points, never multiple competing offers/badges/callouts.
- Hierarchy is driven by EXTREME scale contrast, not by many sizes: a dominant headline (roughly 3-12x the body/legal) states the message, everything else is markedly smaller. Reading path is consistently headline → offer marker → product → logo → legal.
- The headline states the offer/message DIRECTLY and is the single largest element. Set it in the one Dodo geometric grotesque, heavy/black weight, tight leading, flush-left by default (centered only for a one-word product hero). Default to sentence/title case; ALL-CAPS is reserved for type laid over photos.
- Product is real, appetite-forward hero photography — never plated, never on a table, never on a busy/competing background. It is oversized, shot at a slight top-down/three-quarter angle with a soft contact shadow, and either floats as a cut-out on the field or fills the frame as a full-bleed photo. It bleeds off at least one edge and/or is tilted for energy; it is never left flat, centered, or fully contained with empty margins (grid-cell product flyers are the one contained exception).
- The offer/price is ALWAYS a discrete high-contrast device — a rounded container or a headline strike — never loose floating text. The rounded-rectangle/capsule/pill (superellipse) is the house shape.
- Layouts are asymmetric, zoned, and layered: text mass on one side/zone, product opposite; elements overlap zone seams rather than sitting in neat symmetric rows. Never center everything into a tidy stack.
- Backgrounds are flat single-color brand fields, brand gradients, or a full-bleed photo — never plain white paper, never busy patterns, and (on flat/promo variants) never gradients or textures. Atmosphere comes from the product shadow or one clean gradient, not clutter.
- The Dodo logo lockup (dodo-bird-in-orange-square mark + wordmark) is anchored modestly in a corner or footer, sized near body scale, always on the flat field and never over the product. (In-app cards are the one exception: brand is carried by the product artwork itself.)
- A localizable legal/disclaimer line is pinned to the very bottom edge as the quietest element — tiny, tracked, low-contrast, spanning the width. It must be per-market swappable and must never be hardcoded, enlarged, overlap the product/headline, or ship with placeholder text.
- One typeface family does everything; hierarchy comes from weight + scale only. Two weights per layout is the working ceiling (a heavy headline against a regular/medium body). No second font, no serif, no script, no decorative face.
- Generous, consistent margins/safe zones; let the layout breathe. Product and headline dominate; body/subhead and legal stay small and quiet.
- Localization is designed in from the start: headline, offer, and disclaimer are swappable per market (e.g. EN/RU), so text zones and the legal strip are treated as content slots, not baked art.

**Палитра.** "Core Dodo orange is a SEMANTIC accent only — it means 'offer / CTA / channel marker.' Reserve it for the offer device (badge, pill, keyword highlight box, price chip, strike-through, CTA pill) and small accents; never flood the background with it, never use it as decoration or a large fill. Grounds are neutral or brand-saturated, never plain white: cool light gray / off-white (~#EEEFEF) for product-led promo, warm cream (~#F5EEDD) for brand/informational and product flyers, or a brand gradient (deep blue → indigo/violet → warm orange glow) for premium/offer depth; seasonal fields go bold-saturated (e.g. Ramadan blue with an orange light-spill). In-app cards use a warm cream base with pale sage-green organic blobs and forest-green (~#2E3D31) text. Green is reserved strictly for NEW/novelty badges. Secondary palette (teal, mustard, purple, pink) appears only as scattered corner confetti on product-free brand posts or as a single campaign accent (pink/violet) for the offer shape. Text flips for contrast: near-black charcoal on light fields, white/cream on dark or photographic fields. Keep to brand orange + one ground (neutral, cream, or blue/violet gradient) + white + at most one campaign accent and green-for-NEW; do not scatter multiple saturated blocks."

**Типографика.** "Monotypographic across every format: a single Dodo geometric/rounded grotesque superfamily carries all levels — no pairing, no serif, no script, no decorative face. Hierarchy is built from weight and dramatic scale jumps, not from mixing fonts or stacking many sizes; two weights per layout is the practical ceiling. Headline: heavy/black/extra-bold, very large, tight leading, wrapping to 2-4 short lines, sentence or title case by default (ALL-CAPS only when set over a photo; a single word may be knocked out on an orange highlight box, or tinted brand-orange on photos). Body/subhead and product labels: same family, regular/medium/semibold, roughly one-third to one-fifth the headline, sentence case, comfortable leading, high-contrast color. Eyebrow/kicker: same family, small, bold, uppercase or tabular. Legal: same family, tiny, light weight, tracked ALL-CAPS, muted. The one deliberate exception is the CTA badge text, which is white, condensed, uppercase and often italicized for energy; an italic accent cut may also emphasize a single word in informational posts."

**Оффер / цена.** "The commercial hook is expressed with one (or a paired combination) of the house devices, never as loose text, and only ONE primary offer device per layout: (1) spoken in the HEADLINE as plain large type ('35% off', 'only 18 EUR', 'Save 33%'), with the old price shown smaller beneath and cancelled by a SINGLE orange diagonal strike-through; and/or (2) isolated inside one high-contrast rounded container in brand orange (or a campaign accent) — a rounded-rectangle/superellipse pill ('ONLY IN DODO APP', white condensed uppercase italic), a solid-orange keyword highlight box knocking the word out dark-on-orange, a solid-orange filled price chip, a rotated torn-edge orange price block, an outlined/stroked price pill ('от 5 C', 'X€'), or a colored capsule/oval for a discount ('-20%'). The offer shape is the single most saturated object on the layout and typically anchors the center-lower or lower-left. NEW/novelty is a small tilted GREEN badge, kept compact. Prices/discounts stay compact — never blown up as large decorative elements (headline-stated numbers excepted). CTA is understated and app-driven: the orange 'ONLY IN DODO APP' pill, or a QR code plus a short lowercase imperative ('download the app') — never a loud hard button (the in-app payoff card's rounded orange pill CTA is the one exception). The rounded-rect/capsule is the only house badge shape."

**Антипаттерны (чего не делать):**
- Don't put gradients, photographic backgrounds, plates, or tables under promo product shots — food floats on a flat field with only its contact shadow.
- Don't use plain white paper or flat gray/white as a background where the format calls for a brand field — grounds are neutral off-white/cream, brand-saturated, gradient, or photographic.
- Don't box the price/discount in ovals, starbursts, ribbons, or any non-house shape — only the rounded-rect/capsule/pill (and outlined price pill/oval within that family).
- Don't render the offer as loose text without its contrasting container, and don't run more than one primary offer device per layout.
- Don't blow prices/discounts up as large decorative elements — they stay compact chips/pills (unless the number IS the headline).
- Don't set headlines in ALL-CAPS by default, and never introduce a serif, script, or decorative font, a second family, or more than two weights.
- Don't let the product and headline compete as co-equal focal points, and don't center everything into a symmetric stack — layouts are asymmetric and zoned.
- Don't flood the background with orange or scatter multiple saturated color blocks — orange means 'offer' only; keep to one ground plus disciplined accents.
- Don't crowd the layout with multiple offers, badges, or competing callouts — one focal idea per layout.
- Don't leave the product flat, centered, or fully contained with empty margins — tilt it, overlap, and bleed it off at least one edge (contained grid flyers excepted).
- Don't shrink or sideline the hero product, or place it on a busy background that fights the text.
- Don't omit, hardcode, enlarge, or ship placeholder legal text ('disclaimer must be changed…') — pin a tiny, muted, per-market-swappable disclaimer to the bottom edge.
- Don't move the logo off its anchored corner/footer, oversize it, or lay it over the product.
- Don't add loud button-style CTAs; the offer shape and QR/app pill carry the action.
- Don't crowd platform-UI safe zones on stories (~120px top/bottom) or mix two background modes (busy color field AND full photo) in one layout.

## Брифы под форматы

### Instagram Post 1080×1080 (SMM square)

**Бриф для генерации.** Generate a 1080×1080 Instagram square on a flat light-gray (off-white) field. Place one bold, appetite-driven food photo — real Dodo product, no plate, softly shadowed — oversized and bleeding in from the right or bottom corner, occupying roughly 40-55% of the frame. Anchor the message to the opposite (left) edge as a left-aligned stack in a single geometric sans: a very large sentence-case headline that states the offer directly ("35% off", "Super Trio combo only 18 EUR") in near-black, a smaller support line beneath, and where relevant a crossed-out old price with a single orange diagonal strike. Drop the recurring solid-orange rounded-rectangle badge with white condensed uppercase italic "ONLY IN DODO APP" at center-lower, straddling the seam between text and product as the one saturated accent. Lock the dodo-bird + DODO PIZZA logo into a free corner and pin a small low-contrast legal disclaimer along the bottom edge. Keep exactly one focal idea, extreme scale contrast between headline and everything else, and orange used only for the badge and accents. For non-product brand/service messages, switch to a warm cream ground with dark-brown type, an italic-accented headline, and scattered secondary-color confetti shapes in the corners instead of a product photo.

- **Композиция:** Square canvas split into a strong diagonal or left/right axis. The dominant promo layout is a two-zone diagonal: text stack anchored to the LEFT edge (headline block reading top-to-bottom on the left third-to-half), product photography anchored to the opposite corner — either bleeding in from the RIGHT/bottom-right (Super Trio: stacked pizzas cascade from top-right down) or entering from the BOTTOM edge (35% off: pizza cropped by the bottom, secondary item flying in from mid-right). The orange "ONLY IN DODO APP" CTA badge sits in the visual center-to-lower-center, straddling the seam between text and product like a hinge. Logo lockup (dodo bird + "DODO PIZZA" wordmark) lives in a corner: top-left for offer-led posts, bottom-left for product-led posts. A small translucent disclaimer/legal strip pins to the bottom. A distinct variant (informational/brand posts, no product) drops the photo entirely: centered-left text on a cream field framed by scattered decorative confetti/ribbon shapes in brand secondary colors filling the corners.
- **Иерархия:** Ruthless single-focus hierarchy driven by extreme scale contrast. Reading order: (1) giant headline / offer number — the offer itself ("35% off", "Super Trio combo only 18 EUR") is by far the largest element, 3-5x the body; (2) the orange app CTA badge, second loudest via color pop; (3) the product photo competes for attention through size and appetite appeal but sits visually below the headline; (4) supporting body line (conditions, "order over 10 EUR") at ~1/4 the headline size; (5) struck-through old price where applicable; (6) logo, mid-small; (7) legal disclaimer, smallest, near-invisible. One idea dominates each post — no competing focal points.
- **Продукт:** Real, high-appetite hero photography on a seamless white/off-white ground, no visible plate or table — the food floats. Product is oversized and boldly cropped by the frame edge (bleed), never fully contained: pizzas cascade/stack overlapping into the corner, a single item can be hero-cropped at bottom, a secondary item flies diagonally into frame. Soft realistic contact shadow grounds it. Slight top-down / three-quarter angle, cheese-pull and toppings emphasized for craving. Product occupies roughly 40-55% of the canvas and always sits opposite the text mass.
- **Фон:** Flat, near-solid color field — never photographic. Default is a cool light gray / off-white (~#EEEFEF) for product-led promo posts, giving maximum contrast to food and black type. Brand/informational posts use a warm cream (~#F5EEDD) with dark brown type and scattered secondary-palette decorative shapes (teal, mustard, purple, orange, pink confetti/ribbons in corners). No gradients, no textures on promo posts; atmosphere comes only from the product shadow. Orange is reserved strictly for the CTA badge and accents, never the background.
- **Типографика:** One typeface family, geometric sans (Dodo's grotesk), used throughout. Headline: heavy/bold weight, near-black, tight leading, sentence case (not all-caps), very large — set in near-black charcoal on light grounds or brand brown on cream. Body/support: same family, regular/medium weight, much smaller, same color. Badge text is the one exception: white, condensed, uppercase, italicized for energy. Informational variant uses an italic accent cut for the emphasized word ("дольше"). No serifs, no decorative fonts, no more than two weights per layout.
- **Оффер/цена:** Two devices working together: (1) the price/discount is spoken in the HEADLINE as plain large type ("35% off", "only 18 EUR", "Save 33%") — not boxed — with the old price shown smaller beneath and crossed out by a single orange diagonal strike-through; (2) a fixed rounded-rectangle (pill/superellipse) badge in solid brand ORANGE containing white all-caps condensed italic "ONLY IN DODO APP" — the recurring CTA/channel marker, tilted or squared, placed center-lower as the one saturated color object. NEW/flash tags follow the same orange-pill treatment. No ovals or starbursts; the rounded-rect pill is the house shape.
- **Лого/легал:** Logo: dodo-bird glyph in an orange rounded square + black "DODO PIZZA" wordmark, locked together, placed in a corner (top-left for offer-forward posts, bottom-left for product-forward posts), small-to-mid scale, always on the flat background never over the product. Legal/disclaimer: a low-contrast small-caps paragraph (offer conditions, "…ON DODOPIZZA.COM") pinned along the bottom edge, often behind a semi-transparent orange rounded strip; a placeholder note ("The disclaimer must be changed according to the rules of the country") marks where localized legal text is swapped in per market.

**Do:**
- Lead with the offer itself as the single largest element and keep one focal idea per post
- Bleed the product off a frame edge and float it on the flat ground with a soft contact shadow, opposite the text
- Reserve saturated orange for the rounded-rect 'ONLY IN DODO APP' badge and small accents only
- Use one geometric sans family in sentence case, with extreme scale contrast between headline and body
- Show discounts as plain large headline type; strike old prices with a single orange diagonal line
- Keep the logo in a corner on the flat background and pin the localizable legal disclaimer to the bottom edge
- For product-free brand/service posts, use the warm cream ground with brown type and corner confetti shapes

**Don't:**
- Don't use gradients, photographic backgrounds, plates, or tables under promo product shots
- Don't set the headline in all-caps or introduce serif/decorative fonts, or more than two weights
- Don't box the price in ovals, starbursts, or ribbons — the rounded-rect pill is the only house badge shape
- Don't let the product and headline compete as co-equal focal points or center everything symmetrically
- Don't flood the background with orange or scatter multiple saturated color blocks on promo posts
- Don't crowd the layout with multiple offers, badges, or competing callouts
- Don't omit or hardcode the legal disclaimer — leave it as a per-market swappable bottom strip


### Story / Reels 1080×1920 (vertical social)

**Бриф для генерации.** Generate a 1080×1920 vertical Dodo Pizza story split into three overlapping zones. Choose one of two background modes: a saturated brand gradient color-field (blue→purple or orange, with a soft light sweep) carrying a tilted, oversized cut-out of the hero product plus matching 3D themed props; OR a full-bleed appetite-forward food-macro / candid lifestyle photo used as the background. In the top zone place a small bold uppercase eyebrow (date range or qualifier) above a dominant 2–3 line headline in the heavy Dodo display sans — Title Case and white on color fields, ALL-CAPS and brand-orange or black-on-a-yellow-sticker on photos — followed by a small bold sentence-case subhead. Anchor the offer in the lower-left as a distinct high-contrast container (pink capsule pill, rotated torn-edge orange price block, or yellow sticker) holding the discount/NEW/price in extrabold numerals. Reserve ~120px safe margins top and bottom; put the centered orange-dodo + wordmark logo in the footer on photo layouts, with tiny centered uppercase legal text at the very bottom edge. Keep everything monotypographic, drive hierarchy through steep scale contrast and color-field isolation of the offer, and let the hero image bleed past at least one frame edge.

- **Композиция:** Three horizontal zones on the 9:16 canvas. TOP zone (~0–35%): headline block, left-aligned or centered, with a small eyebrow line (date range / qualifier) directly above it. MIDDLE zone (~30–75%): the hero — either a cut-out product photo floating and rotated with themed 3D props, or a full-bleed food/lifestyle image that occupies the entire frame behind the text. BOTTOM-LEFT / LOWER zone (~55–90%): the offer object (discount pill, price block, or offer line). FOOTER (~90–100%): centered Dodo logo lockup and, when required, a one-line legal disclaimer at the very bottom edge. Generous top/bottom safe margins (~120px) are left clear for platform UI. Layout is deliberately asymmetric and layered — elements overlap zone boundaries rather than sitting in neat rows.
- **Иерархия:** Steep three-step scale contrast: (1) the headline dominates, set in a heavy display sans at roughly 130–200px, wrapping to 2–3 short lines; (2) the offer/price is the second-loudest element, isolated inside a colored container so it reads as a separate "stamp"; (3) the body subhead and eyebrow are small (~45–70px) and quiet. Reading order is headline → hero image → offer → logo. Contrast is driven by size and by color-field isolation, not by many type sizes.
- **Продукт:** Two treatments recur. (a) Cut-out mode: the product is masked out of its background, enlarged past frame edges, tilted ~10–20°, and layered so multiple items overlap each other; complementary 3D-rendered props (music notes, microphone) share the same lighting and sit around it. (b) Full-bleed mode: a single macro food shot or a candid lifestyle photo (person eating/holding product) fills the entire canvas and doubles as the background, with text laid directly over it. In both cases the food is shot close, appetite-forward, and bleeds off at least one edge.
- **Фон:** Two options, pick one per layout: (1) a saturated brand color field — smooth blue-to-purple or orange gradient with a soft light sweep for depth — used when the product is a floating cut-out; or (2) the hero photo itself (food macro or lifestyle) as an edge-to-edge full-bleed, with text placed on its higher-contrast regions. No flat neutral/gray backgrounds; the field is always brand-saturated or photographic.
- **Типографика:** One family does all the work: the Dodo heavy geometric/condensed display sans. Headline is maximum weight (black/extrabold), tight leading, set in Title Case (color-field layouts) or ALL-CAPS (photo layouts), and can be tinted brand-orange when sitting on a photo. Body subhead is the same family at bold, sentence case, small. Offer numerals are the same family, extrabold. Eyebrow (date/qualifier) is small, bold, uppercase or tabular. No secondary/serif font — the whole layout is monotypographic with weight and scale carrying the contrast.
- **Оффер/цена:** Always a distinct high-contrast container, never loose text. Patterns seen: a rounded-capsule "pill" in a contrasting brand accent (pink) holding a bold white "-20%"; a rectangular orange block, slightly rotated with a torn/brush edge, stacking a small "FROM" label over a huge price ("XX€"); or the offer folded into a large yellow rounded-rectangle sticker that also carries the headline. NEW/discount/price is always the loudest colored object after the headline and typically anchors the lower-left.
- **Лого/легал:** Dodo logo — orange dodo-head mark plus white "DODO PIZZA" wordmark — placed centered in the footer band on photo/full-bleed layouts; it may be omitted on in-app color-field stories where brand context is already implied. Legal/disclaimer sits as tiny centered uppercase text pinned to the very bottom edge, spanning the width, low-emphasis so it never competes with the offer.

**Do:**
- Pick ONE background mode per layout — brand-gradient-with-cutout, or full-bleed photo — and commit to it
- Make the headline dominate with a heavy display sans wrapping to 2–3 short lines
- Isolate the price/discount inside its own contrasting colored shape (pill, block, or sticker)
- Bleed the hero product/photo past at least one edge and tilt cut-outs for energy
- Keep a single type family; use weight and scale, not a second font, for contrast
- Match any 3D props to the product's lighting and the campaign theme
- Leave ~120px top/bottom safe zones clear for platform UI
- Anchor the offer lower-left and the logo/legal in the footer

**Don't:**
- Don't use a flat gray/white background — the field is always brand-saturated or photographic
- Don't set the offer as loose text without a colored container
- Don't center everything into a neat symmetric stack — the layout is asymmetric and layered
- Don't introduce a second/serif font or many type sizes
- Don't let body copy or legal text rival the headline or offer in size
- Don't leave the hero product fully inside the frame with visible empty margins around it
- Don't crowd the extreme top/bottom edges where platform UI overlaps
- Don't mix both background modes (busy color field AND full photo) in one layout


### TV board 1920×1080 (horizontal digital menu/promo) — Dodo Pizza

**Бриф для генерации.** Generate a 1920×1080 horizontal Dodo Pizza TV promo as a two-zone split: a left message column (~45% width) on a flat field — warm off-white/pale tint by default, or a bold saturated campaign color for seasonal — and a right zone (~55%) filled by one large, appetite-driven hero product shot (in-brand packaging), either edge-bleeding as a cutout or contained in a big rounded-rectangle photo mask with margin. In the message column, stack, top to bottom, the Dodo logo lockup, an oversized extra-bold Dodo-grotesk headline in tight-leading sentence/title case (2-4 lines, charcoal on light / white on dark), a smaller regular-weight body line beneath, and a whisper-quiet uppercase gray legal line at the bottom. Add exactly one orange offer accent — a tilted rounded orange badge with white uppercase text, or an orange highlight box behind a keyword, or an outlined price pill — as the single saturated pop against otherwise monochrome type. Keep one type family throughout, strong scale contrast between headline and body, generous consistent margins, and let orange mean "offer" only.

- **Композиция:** Two-zone horizontal split down roughly the vertical midline. One half (usually left, occasionally right) is the message column: logo + headline + body/offer, all left-aligned and vertically centered or top-aligned. The other half is a single dominant product image. The product half either bleeds a cutout product to the frame edge on a flat field (Free Rolls) or holds a rounded-corner photo panel floating with margin inside a colored field (Reusable Case). Generous uniform outer margin (~5-6% of width) frames the message column. On bold-color variants (Ramadan) the split can stack vertically with headline top and product filling the lower two-thirds, but the horizontal 50/50 text-vs-product split is the canonical layout. A logo lockup anchors either the top-left corner (full DODO PIZZA wordmark) or sits directly above the disclaimer at the bottom of the text column.
- **Иерархия:** Three clean tiers with strong scale contrast. Tier 1: an oversized headline (roughly 110-150px cap height, ~7-12% of frame height) that is unmistakably the loudest element. Tier 2: a supporting body/subhead at roughly one-third the headline size, regular weight, sitting immediately below with a clear gap. Tier 3: the offer/price device (badge, highlight, or price pill) reads as a punctuating accent, plus a whisper-quiet legal line at the very bottom. Reading order: logo → headline → offer emphasis → body → legal. The product photo carries equal visual weight to the headline through sheer size, so the eye ping-pongs between headline and food.
- **Продукт:** Single hero product shot, appetite-forward, shot on-brand (in packaging: kraft box, reusable orange case, ceramic bowl). Two placement modes: (a) silhouetted/edge-bleeding cutout that occupies ~45-55% of the frame width on the product side, cropped so it slightly exceeds the frame; or (b) a contained photo inside a large rounded-rectangle mask with visible margin on the colored field. Product is large — it fills most of its half — and positioned slightly off-center toward the outer edge, tilted or angled for energy. Never small, never centered as an afterthought, never on a busy background that competes with the text side.
- **Фон:** Flat single-color field, no gradients on the light variants. Default is a near-white warm off-white or pale desaturated tint (pale green in Reusable Case) that lets the product and dark type pop. Seasonal/campaign variants use a bold saturated field (deep Ramadan blue with a warm orange light-spill plane) and may add subtle brand motifs (large ghosted rounded-shape watermark, small orange sparkle glyphs). Text color flips accordingly: near-black charcoal on light fields, white on dark fields. Orange is reserved for the offer accent, not the background.
- **Типографика:** Single Dodo brand grotesk family throughout. Headline in black/extra-bold weight, tight leading, left-aligned, 2-4 short lines, sentence or title case. Body in regular/medium of the same family with open line spacing. Emphasis via weight, orange highlight box, or color flip — never a second typeface. Legal in small tracked uppercase gray. Headline cap height roughly 3x the body.
- **Оффер/цена:** One primary offer device per layout, always in Dodo orange, and it is the only saturated accent on otherwise restrained typography. Three recurring treatments: (1) a rounded-corner orange blob/pill badge with white bold uppercase text set at a slight jaunty tilt ("ONLY IN DODO APP"); (2) an orange solid highlight box painted behind one or two keywords of the headline so the word is knocked-out dark-on-orange ("extra cashback"); (3) a price in an outlined/stroked pill or oval ("от 5 C") with no fill. Prices/conditions ("with order over 15 EUR") sit as plain body text under the headline. CTA is implicit/app-driven, not a hard button.
- **Лого/легал:** Logo: Dodo dodo-head-in-orange-square mark plus wordmark, either the full horizontal DODO PIZZA lockup top-left, or the compact "dodo pizza" lockup sitting just above the disclaimer at the bottom of the text column. Sized modestly (~logo height ≈ body text scale), never competing with the headline. Legal/disclaimer: bottom-left aligned to the text column (or bottom edge), tiny uppercase tracked gray caps, 1-3 lines, containing validity dates and conditions. It is deliberately the quietest element and must never overlap the product or headline.

**Do:**
- Use one dominant, well-lit hero product shot in on-brand packaging, sized to fill its half of the frame
- Keep a hard two-zone split: quiet flat field for the message column, product on the other side
- Reserve Dodo orange for a single offer accent (badge, keyword highlight, or price pill)
- Set a giant extra-bold headline with tight leading against a much smaller regular-weight body
- Left-align the whole message column and hold generous consistent margins
- Anchor the logo modestly (top-left lockup or above the disclaimer) and keep the legal line tiny and muted
- Flip type color to stay high-contrast on the chosen field (charcoal on light, white on dark)

**Don't:**
- Don't center everything or scatter multiple competing accent colors — orange means offer only
- Don't shrink the product or place it on a busy background that fights the text
- Don't mix multiple font families or use serif/script for emphasis
- Don't add more than one primary offer device per layout
- Don't let the legal disclaimer grow large, overlap the product, or leave placeholder text (e.g. 'disclaimer must be changed') in the final
- Don't use gradients or drop-shadow clutter on the flat light variants
- Don't crowd the headline with loose leading or long body paragraphs


### Leaflet A6 105×148 mm (print flyer / giftcard) — Dodo Pizza

**Бриф для генерации.** Generate a portrait A6 (105×148 mm) Dodo Pizza leaflet as a single full-bleed color field — either a smooth brand blue→violet→warm gradient (giftcard/offer) or a warm cream/peach solid (product flyer). Stack content in a strict vertical spine with generous top and bottom padding: a small tight-tracked eyebrow label at top, then a massive two-line headline in the brand's bold rounded sans that spans nearly edge to edge and owns the upper half. Below it place ONE mechanic zone: for a giftcard, a full-width rounded outline capsule containing a monospaced-feeling promocode with a small lowercase caption under it; for a product offer, a uniform 2×3 grid of cut-out product hero shots on transparent/paper, each with a bold product name and a solid-orange "XX€" price chip, optionally with a small tilted green NEW badge by the headline. Close with a footer lockup: the Dodo Pizza dodo-bird logo (white or orange) at bottom-left, an optional white QR card at bottom-right, and a block of small all-caps legal fine print pinned to the very bottom. Use only brand orange, blue/violet gradient, white, and one green accent for NEW; keep headline white on dark fields and orange on light fields for maximum contrast.
